# University of Moratuwa (UoM) - Overview and Faculties

## General Overview and Location
The University of Moratuwa (informally known as Moratuwa University or UoM) is a prestigious state university in Sri Lanka, renowned for its engineering, technological, architectural, and business education.
- Location: Katubedda, Moratuwa 10400, Western Province, Sri Lanka.
- Setting: Urban/suburban campus located alongside the Bolgoda Lake, easily accessible via the Galle Road (A2 highway).
- Historical Roots: Traces its lineage back to the Ceylon Technical College established in 1893, later becoming the Institute of Practical Technology (IPT) Katubedda in 1960, the Katubedda Campus of the University of Ceylon in 1972, and gaining autonomous status as the University of Moratuwa in 1978.

## Faculties of the University of Moratuwa
The university comprises five major degree-awarding undergraduate faculties and a Faculty of Graduate Studies:

### 1. Faculty of Engineering
The Faculty of Engineering is the oldest and largest engineering faculty in Sri Lanka. It produces world-class graduates recognized internationally under the Washington Accord.
Departments in the Faculty of Engineering include:
- Department of Computer Science & Engineering (CSE): Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Computer Science & Engineering, and Integrated Computer Engineering.
- Department of Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering (ENTC): Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Electronic & Telecommunication Engineering, and Biomedical Engineering.
- Department of Electrical Engineering (EE): Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Electrical Engineering.
- Department of Mechanical Engineering: Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Mechanical Engineering.
- Department of Civil Engineering: Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Civil Engineering.
- Department of Chemical & Process Engineering (CPE): Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Chemical & Process Engineering.
- Department of Materials Science & Engineering (MSE): Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Materials Science & Engineering.
- Department of Earth Resources Engineering (ERE): Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Earth Resources Engineering.
- Department of Textile & Apparel Engineering: Offers BSc Engineering Honours in Textile & Apparel Engineering, and BSc in Fashion Design & Product Development.
- Department of Transport & Logistics Management (TLM): Offers BSc Honours in Transport & Logistics Management.
- Department of Mathematics: Serves mathematics education across all engineering streams.

### 2. Faculty of Information Technology (FIT)
Established in 2001, FIT is the first and only dedicated IT faculty in the Sri Lankan state university system.
Departments:
- Department of Information Technology
- Department of Computational Mathematics
- Department of Interdisciplinary Studies
Undergraduate degree programs offered:
- Bachelor of Science Honours in Information Technology (BSc Hons IT)
- Bachelor of Science Honours in Information Technology & Management (BSc Hons ITM)
- Bachelor of Science Honours in Artificial Intelligence (BSc Hons AI)

### 3. Faculty of Architecture
Renowned as the premier design and built-environment education centre in Sri Lanka, accredited by RIBA (Royal Institute of British Architects) and RICS (Royal Institution of Chartered Surveyors).
Departments:
- Department of Architecture (Bachelor of Architecture - B.Arch)
- Department of Building Economics (BSc Hons in Quantity Surveying, BSc Hons in Facilities Management)
- Department of Town & Country Planning (BSc Hons in Town & Country Planning)
- Department of Integrated Design (Bachelor of Design - B.Des)

### 4. Faculty of Business
Established in 2017 to foster technology-driven business management professionals.
Departments:
- Department of Management of Technology
- Department of Decision Sciences
- Department of Industrial Management
Degree programs offered:
- Bachelor of Business Science Honours (BBSc Hons) specializing in Business Analytics, Financial Analytics, and Business Process Management.

### 5. Faculty of Medicine
Established in 2020 as the newest faculty, with clinical training situated at the Colombo South Teaching Hospital and Nagoda General Hospital, Kalutara.
Undergraduate program:
- Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery (MBBS).

### 6. Faculty of Graduate Studies (FGS)
Coordinates multi-disciplinary postgraduate studies, Master of Science (MSc), Master of Philosophy (MPhil), and Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programs across all academic disciplines.
