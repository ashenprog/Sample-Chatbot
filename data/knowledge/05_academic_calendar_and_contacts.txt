University of Moratuwa - Academic Calendar and Contact Directory

1. Academic Calendar Structure:
The academic year at University of Moratuwa is divided into two regular semesters of approximately 15 to 18 weeks each:
- Semester 1: Typically starts in the first half of the year (usually February/March), comprising 14-15 weeks of academic lectures/labs, a 1-week mid-semester recess, a 2-week study leave, and 2-3 weeks of end-semester written and practical examinations.
- Semester 2: Typically runs from August/September through December/January following the same 15-week lecture and examination rhythm.
- Industrial Training: Undergraduate engineering, IT, and architecture curricula require a mandatory 24 to 28-week industrial training internship period, typically scheduled during the 5th or 6th semester.
- Vacations: Scheduled short term breaks between semesters, national public holidays, and Sinhala/Tamil New Year festive breaks.
Note: Academic calendars may be adjusted or shifted depending on national examination timetables, public health directives, or faculty senate resolutions. Students must always consult the official Faculty timetable noticeboards or the Learner Management System (Moodle) for their specific batch calendar.

3. Getting to the University:
- By Public Bus: Take any bus along the Colombo-Galle Road (Route 100, 101, 400, 430, 02) to the Katubedda Junction. The university entrance is approximately 500 meters down Bandaranayake Mawatha from Katubedda Junction.
- By Train: The closest railway stations are Moratuwa Railway Station (approx. 3.5 km) and Lunawa Railway Station (approx. 2.5 km), with frequent tuk-tuks and local bus connections to Katubedda.
