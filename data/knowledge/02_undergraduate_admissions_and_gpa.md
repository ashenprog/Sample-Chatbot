# Undergraduate Admissions and GPA System - University of Moratuwa

## Undergraduate Admission Process
Admission to state universities in Sri Lanka, including the University of Moratuwa, is centralized and administered by the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Sri Lanka (https://ugc.ac.lk).

### Admission Channels and Requirements:
1. GCE Advanced Level (A/L) Stream Requirements:
   - Engineering Faculty: Applicants must sit for the G.C.E. Advanced Level in the Physical Science (Maths) stream with Combined Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry. Admission is based on Z-Score rankings under national merit and district quota systems.
   - Information Technology Faculty: Applicants can enter from multiple streams (Physical Science, Biological Science, Commerce, Arts, or Technology stream) meeting UGC criteria.
   - Architecture Faculty: Applicants must qualify in specified A/L subjects and MUST pass the University Aptitude Test conducted by the University of Moratuwa for Architecture, Design, Fashion Design & Product Development, or Landscape Architecture.
   - Business Faculty: Applicants qualify via the Commerce, Biological Science, or Physical Science streams as stipulated in the UGC handbook.
   - Medicine Faculty: Applicants must qualify in the Biological Science stream (Biology, Chemistry, and Physics) with requisite Z-scores.

2. Application Steps:
   - Following the release of GCE A/L examination results, students submit their online university preferences through the official UGC portal (UGC Handbook application).
   - If applying for degree courses requiring aptitude tests (Architecture, Design, Fashion Design), candidates must register separately for the aptitude test through the University of Moratuwa admissions portal when advertised.
   - Selected candidates receive official admission letters from the UGC and registration packages from the University of Moratuwa Academic & Admissions Division.

3. Foreign Students:
   - A limited number of places are allocated for foreign students or Sri Lankan students with foreign qualifications (e.g., Cambridge / Edexcel London A/Levels) through UGC foreign admission schemes.

## Credit and Grading System (GPA)
The University of Moratuwa operates on a modular credit-based semester system.

### Grade Point Scale:
Academic performance is calculated using Grade Point Average (GPA) on a 4.00 scale:
- Grade A+ : 4.00 Grade Points (Outstanding)
- Grade A  : 4.00 Grade Points (Excellent)
- Grade A- : 3.70 Grade Points
- Grade B+ : 3.30 Grade Points
- Grade B  : 3.00 Grade Points (Good)
- Grade B- : 2.70 Grade Points
- Grade C+ : 2.30 Grade Points
- Grade C  : 2.00 Grade Points (Satisfactory / Pass grade)
- Grade C- : 1.50 Grade Points
- Grade D  : 1.00 Grade Point (Conditional pass)
- Grade I  : Incomplete
- Grade F  : 0.00 Grade Points (Fail)

### Minimum Degree and Class Honours Requirements:
To be eligible for the award of an undergraduate Honours degree, a candidate must:
- Successfully earn the minimum required academic credits specified by the faculty curriculum (usually ~135-150 credits for 4-year engineering/architecture/IT degrees).
- Maintain an Overall Grade Point Average (OGPA) of at least 2.00.
- Have no unresolved 'F' grades in compulsory courses.

Class Honours Award Criteria:
- First Class Honours: Overall GPA of 3.70 or higher.
- Second Class Honours (Upper Division): Overall GPA of 3.30 to 3.69.
- Second Class Honours (Lower Division): Overall GPA of 3.00 to 3.29.
- Pass Degree: Overall GPA of 2.00 to 2.99.
*(Note: Candidate must also complete the degree within the normal stipulated time period without academic repeats to qualify for Honours).*
