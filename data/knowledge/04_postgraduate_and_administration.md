# Postgraduate Studies and University Administration - University of Moratuwa

## Postgraduate Degree Programs
The University of Moratuwa offers postgraduate diplomas, Master of Science (MSc), Master of Business Administration (MBA), Master of Philosophy (MPhil), and Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) programs across its faculties.

### Key Postgraduate Programs by Faculty:
- Faculty of Engineering:
  - MSc / PG Diploma in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering.
  - MSc / PG Diploma in Telecommunications, Electronics & Automation.
  - MSc in Electrical Engineering, Electrical Installations, Power Systems.
  - MSc in Structural Engineering, Highway & Traffic Engineering, Water Resources Engineering.
  - MSc in Sustainable Process Engineering, Materials Science, Mining & Mineral Exploration.
  - MBA in Supply Chain Management, Project Management, and Industrial Management.
- Faculty of Information Technology:
  - MSc / PG Diploma in Information Technology.
  - MSc in Artificial Intelligence.
  - Master of Science in Cloud Computing & Enterprise Solutions.
- Faculty of Architecture:
  - Master of Architecture (M.Arch), Master of Urban Design (MUD), MSc in Town & Country Planning, MSc in Project Management, MSc in Occupational Safety & Health.
- Faculty of Business:
  - MBA in Management of Technology (MOT), MBA in Entrepreneurship, Executive MBA.
- Faculty of Graduate Studies (FGS):
  - Multidisciplinary research degrees: Master of Philosophy (MPhil) and Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) in all cutting-edge technological and scientific disciplines.

### General Admission Requirements for Postgraduate Studies:
- Master by Coursework / PG Diploma: Recognised Bachelor's degree (Honours) in the relevant field or an equivalent professional qualification (e.g., IESL, BCS, CIMA, RIBA) plus minimum industrial experience as stipulated by the department.
- Research Degrees (MPhil / PhD): Strong undergraduate record (typically First or Second Class Upper Honours) or a Master's degree with a demonstrated research component. Submission of an approved research proposal and identification of an academic supervisor.

## University Administration and Governance
The University of Moratuwa is governed under the Universities Act No. 16 of 1978 of Sri Lanka.

### Principal Officers of Governance:
- Chancellor: The titular head of the university who presides over general convocations for the conferment of degrees.
- Vice Chancellor (VC): The chief executive officer and academic head of the university responsible for day-to-day academic administration and strategic leadership.
- Deputy Vice Chancellor: Assists the Vice Chancellor in academic, student, and administrative matters.
- Deans of Faculties: Executive heads of each respective faculty (Engineering, Architecture, Information Technology, Business, Medicine, Graduate Studies).
- Registrar: Head of general administration, custodian of records, and secretary to the Council and Senate.
- Bursar: Chief financial officer responsible for university budgeting and financial management.

### Note on Time-Sensitive Leadership Positions:
Administrative officeholders, including the Vice Chancellor, Deans, Registrar, and Council members, serve specified fixed terms (typically 3-year cycles) and change periodically upon election, appointment, or reappointment.
- Local static handbooks and offline documents may reflect past officeholders (for example, historically Prof. Malik Ranasinghe, Prof. Ananda Jayawardane, Prof. Kapila Perera, Prof. Niranjan Gunawardena).
- CAUTION: Because administrative appointments change over time, always refer directly to the live official university portal at https://uom.lk/about/officers for the verified, currently serving Vice Chancellor and university officers.
