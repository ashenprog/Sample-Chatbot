# University of Moratuwa - Facilities, Hostels, and Student Life

## Student Hostels and Accommodation
The University of Moratuwa provides on-campus and near-campus hostel accommodation for eligible undergraduate students, administered by the Student Welfare Division.

### Hostel Allocation Criteria:
- Eligibility: Priority is granted to First-Year students during their initial orientation/academic period, and Final-Year students completing their capstone graduation projects.
- Distance Rule: Students residing beyond a designated threshold distance (typically > 40-50 km from Moratuwa) are prioritized.
- Medical or Special Needs: Special accommodations are allocated on medical grounds or extreme socio-economic grounds upon review by the University Medical Officer (UMO) and Welfare Committee.
- Major University Hostels:
  - Boys' Hostels: Rahula Hostel, Captain Dawson Hostel, Bimsara Hostel, and outside leased hostels in Katubedda.
  - Girls' Hostels: Girls' Hostel Complex (Katubedda campus), Maradha Hostel, and university-administered annexes.
- Private Boarding Places: Because on-campus hostel capacity cannot house 100% of the student population, many second and third-year students find private boarding places (chummeries/rooms) in Katubedda, Moratumulla, Lunawa, and Mount Lavinia within walking distance or a short bus ride.

## Campus Facilities and Amenities
- Library: A multi-story central library offering extensive physical book collections, electronic research databases (IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect), individual study carrels, reading halls, and photocopy services.
- Health Centre: The University Medical Centre provides free basic medical consultations, emergency care, dental treatment, and referrals for students and staff.
- Sports and Gymnasium:
  - Full-size floodlit grounds for cricket, rugby, soccer, and athletics.
  - Modern gymnasium with fitness equipment, badminton courts, basketball courts, and table tennis.
  - 25-meter Olympic-standard swimming pool.
  - Tennis courts and netball courts.
- Canteens and Dining: Student canteens (L-Canteen, Staff Canteen, Architecture Canteen, FIT Canteen) serving subsidized meals, snacks, and beverages.

## Student Societies, Clubs, and Extracurriculars
Moratuwa has a vibrant, student-led extracurricular culture spanning technical, cultural, sporting, and social service organizations:
- Rotaract Club of University of Moratuwa (Rotaract Mora): Award-winning community service and leadership youth organization known nationally and internationally for large-scale social welfare projects.
- IEEE Student Branch of University of Moratuwa: Highly active IEEE student chapter organizing hackathons (such as MoraXtreme, IEEEXtreme training), workshops, and technical symposiums.
- E-Club (Electronic & Telecommunication Club): Renowned for organizing the SLRC (Sri Lanka Robotics Challenge), bringing together roboticists from schools and universities island-wide.
- Mora Hiking Club: Sri Lanka's pioneer university hiking community, organizing exploratory trekking, mountaineering, and environmental conservation trails across the island.
- MoraSpirit: Leading student media and sports journalism initiative providing live coverage of Sri Lanka University Games (SLUG) and inter-university championships.
- AIESEC in University of Moratuwa: Facilitates international youth exchange, leadership development, and global volunteering internships.
- Building Economics Students' Society (BESS), Architecture Students' Association (ASA), and Computer Science & Engineering Society (CSES): Field-specific academic and industrial networking societies.
- Religious and Cultural Societies: Buddhist Brotherhood, Catholic Students' Movement, Hindu Students' Union, Muslim Majlis, Sinhala Cultural Society, and English Literary Association.
